Loose Ends

Say it. Clear your head.

Tasks show up while you are busy with something else. Loose Ends takes them in one sentence, sorts them on your device and lays them out like a page in a good notebook: calm, clear, made with care.

Download on the App StoreConnect Claude
Why I built it

I built it for two reasons. The first: the tasks I lose are the ones that occur to me on the way, on a walk, at the checkout, halfway through an email. By the time I had opened a to-do app and picked a date, a list and a priority, the thought was gone. So Loose Ends asks for one sentence and nothing else, keeps it exactly as I said it and works out the rest by itself.

The second: every to-do app I tried looked like a database. Rows, boxes, badges, a dozen colors shouting at me. I open my task list many times a day, and I wanted to enjoy looking at it. A list I like to open is a list I actually use.

Made to be looked at

So I cared as much about how Loose Ends looks as about what it does. It should feel like a good notebook: paper instead of cards, type you want to read, the time in the margin like a diary, and color only where it means something. Red is time pressure, green is the moment you finish. When you complete a task, a small knot ties itself. That is all the celebration it needs.

How it works
1
Say it

Type or dictate one sentence: in the app, on Apple Watch, from a widget, the Action button or the share sheet.

2
It sorts itself

Due date, duration, importance and tag come from your sentence. Fixed rules first, Apple Intelligence on your device only where language needs understanding.

3
It shows up when it fits

“Now” holds what fits this moment. Reminders come on the due day, or when you arrive at a place.

New in 1.0.2
Loose Ends and Claude

Ask Claude what's on today, plan your day together, or turn an email into tasks. Claude works only with what Loose Ends allows: it reads, captures, completes, reschedules and adds steps. It cannot delete anything. Every change runs on your own device, and the task shows you what Claude changed.

  1. In Loose Ends, open Settings › Claude and sign in with Apple.
  2. In Claude, open Settings › Connectors › Add custom connector. Name it Loose Ends and enter https://loose-ends.henemm.com/mcp.
  3. Sign in with Apple once more and choose Allow access.
